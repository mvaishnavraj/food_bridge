The 7-Step Lifecycle Formula

POST ➔ VERIFY ➔ PRIORITIZE ➔ MATCH ➔ PICKUP ➔ DELIVER ➔ MEASURE

🤖 AI / VLM Role (Ollama + LLaVA)

What it does: Preliminary visual verification of bulk/commercial surplus vessels vs. individual leftover plates.

⚠️ Defense Tip: Do NOT claim AI certifies food bacteriological safety. Frame it as visual volume validation.

🌡️ Quality & Urgency Scoring

Formula: $S = S_{\text{time}} (40) + S_{\text{perishability}} (25) + S_{\text{weather}} (15) + S_{\text{qty}} (10) + S_{\text{freshness}} (10)$

Real-time Open-Meteo API factors in temperature & humidity decay.

🎯 Intelligent Matching Algorithm

Formula: $\text{Match} = 0.35 \times D_{\text{dist}} + 0.25 \times C_{\text{type}} + 0.20 \times Q_{\text{cov}} + 0.20 \times U_{\text{urgency}}$

Coordinates supply and demand within feasible travel radius.

🚴 Volunteer & Transport Model

Surplus food is free. The platform computes transparent distance-based transport estimates (₹) borne by the accepting party.

Key Viva Q&A
Q: Why is this not just a simple CRUD website?

A: Because FoodBridge coordinates a multi-agent system with real-time VLM verification, weather decay heuristics, geospatial distance routing, and delivery audit logging.

Q: How do you compute meal-equivalents?

A: Standard food science approximation factors ($1\text{ kg cooked meal} \approx 3.0\text{ meals}$, $1\text{ kg food rescued} \approx 2.5\text{ kg CO}_2\text{ avoided}$).
